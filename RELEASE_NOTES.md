# FlyTS-Mini v0.1 Research Preview

Status: archived engineering candidate; publication stopped at M10B

Former candidate tag (not created): `flyts-mini-v0.1-preview.1`
Python package: `flyts 0.2.0`

## What this preview provides

- Source for the variable-channel FlyTS engineering MVP.
- Shared tokenizer/router and swappable fly-like, rewired, random, dense-leaky and
  GRU engineering paths.
- Leakage-aware masking, public corpus preparation, CPU pretraining, validation,
  checkpoint save/load/resume, embedding and evaluator paths.
- Exact release-supported configs, documentation, tests, source/package hashes,
  a synthetic fixture and checkpoint-free offline verification instructions.
- A prospective self-contained reporting contract for future preserved inputs.
- MIT-licensed FlyTS source and package metadata; dataset rights remain separate.

## What this preview does not provide

- No pretrained checkpoint, selected model or model-performance guarantee.
- No completed formal comparison or final-held-out Electricity evaluation.
- No validated topology advantage, robustness improvement, foundation quality or
  transfer result.
- No CUDA validation or semiconductor suitability evidence.
- No raw datasets, run outputs, embeddings, target-unspecified wheelhouse or
  private/company artifact.
- No PyPI publication.
- Historical `reports/PILOT_RESULTS.json` and `docs/BASELINE_VALIDATION.md` remain
  byte-preserved in Git but are excluded from new release archives because they
  contain personal absolute paths. This does not erase existing Git history.

## Research status

Stage 08 produced one-seed operational pilot evidence with independent QA `PASS`.
It is descriptive and cannot rank or select a model. Stage 09 closed at `HOLD`;
its final independent QA is `FAIL`. The proposed 60-run protocol was not frozen or
executed, numerical rules were not frozen, H-01–H-03 have no formal result, H-04
was not tested and final-held-out evidence remained sealed.

Stage 10 prospective reporter hardening applies only to future release code. It
does not modify, supersede or retroactively pass Stage 09 evidence.
