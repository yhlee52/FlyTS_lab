## Proposed message

To: `contact@physionet.org`  
Subject: EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset v1.0.0 — individual-content rights for nonpublic encoder evaluation

Hello PhysioNet team,

We are planning a nonpublic academic evaluation of a multivariate time-series
encoder using a subject-disjoint development subset of the EEG Motor
Movement/Imagery Dataset v1.0.0 (DOI `10.13026/C28G6P`). The planned use is to
read EEG signals, construct paired subsets of the 64 channels, and calculate
aggregate development metrics. We would not use these subjects for encoder
pretraining or release raw recordings, derived signal arrays, subject-level
outputs, or model checkpoints. No formal subject split or further signal
access has been approved. An audit-only pilot inspected six recordings from
three subjects in memory; those subjects are excluded from all later
pretraining, development and final/test use.

The dataset page lists open access under ODC-By v1.0. The license explains
that individual database contents may have separate copyright, privacy or
data-protection rights. Could you please clarify:

1. Whether the posted access and license terms permit the proposed nonpublic
   processing of the individual EEG recordings for encoder development-only
   evaluation, or whether additional contributor permission, consent/ethics
   review, privacy conditions or an institutional data-use agreement applies.
2. Whether creating and retaining access-controlled internal derived views,
   aggregate metrics and private checkpoints from this use has any separate
   conditions. We would seek a further review before publishing metrics or
   distributing any weights or data derivatives.
3. Which attribution, license notice and source citation requirements would
   apply to internal records and a later research paper or public artifact.
4. If your team cannot determine individual-content rights for this dataset,
   who is the appropriate contributor or rights contact for this question.

We can provide a more specific protocol or institutional details if needed.
Thank you for directing us to the applicable conditions.

[PI name, institution, reply address]
