# Stage 02 masking

Training accepts exactly one of legacy `mask_ratio` or `masking` with `temporal_ratio`, `channel_ratio`, and `channel_dropout_ratio`. Legacy configuration is interpreted as temporal-only for validation and resume comparison; existing JSON and version-1 checkpoint formats remain unchanged. Ratios are finite in `[0,1)`; temporal plus channel masking must be positive, and channel masking plus dropout must be below one.

The sampler returns independent `[B,P,C]` temporal, channel, and dropout causes. Dropout is drawn first and removes input only. Temporal masking retains the original patch sampler and seed stream. Channel targets are drawn only from observed, non-dropped channels. A sampled channel owns any temporal overlap, and the union counts each observed position once. Missing and padded positions are never targets. Seeded stochastic rounding determines channel counts and may select zero when the expected count is small; capacity and visible-context guards do not force a target. Single-channel channel masking and dropout do nothing. A channel-only configuration can therefore produce a target-free batch, particularly when all records have one channel. Training raises a clear error and recommends a positive temporal ratio; model forward remains available.

The encoder keeps observed, missing, padding, visible, temporal target, channel target, and dropout masks separate at sample/patch level. `collate_windows` provides `channel_counts` alongside lengths so batch channel padding is distinct from real NaN missingness. Callers that omit `channel_counts` declare every supplied channel real; NaNs in them are missing. A hidden or dropped value never enters input tokens or normalization. Channels with visible samples use their own visible mean and scale. A fully hidden channel uses pooled visible statistics from its record; this also applies to a channel with no visible samples for another reason. Manually supplied `MaskPlan`s must use full-channel channel/dropout causes, keep those causes disjoint, and retain an originally observed visible sample per record. Reconstruction uses Smooth L1 with `beta=1.0`. Validation samples temporal and channel targets but has no dropout. Fixed independent seed offsets are temporal train `+10000+epoch`, temporal validation `+20000`, channel train `+30000+epoch`, dropout train `+40000+epoch`, and channel validation `+50000`.

The encoder is channel permutation equivariant under a paired permutation of input and masks. Fully hidden channels without identities or metadata remain indistinguishable; Stage 02 does not add channel IDs or tokens. CPU epoch-boundary resume is verified; CUDA behavior awaits hardware validation. These tests establish mechanics and leakage guards, not representation quality or dropout robustness.
